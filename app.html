      How was this prediction calculated?

      Every answer adds or subtracts weight from each of the four candidate diseases for your crop. The weights come from standard symptom descriptions of those diseases. Scores are then converted into a percentage share, and the headline confidence blends that share with how much of the available evidence you confirmed (capped at 90%). The same answers always produce the same result. This is a deterministic scoring prototype — it is not a trained machine-learning model and its accuracy has not been validated.
